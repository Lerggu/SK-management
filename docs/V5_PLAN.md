# V5 — Lifting & Material Flow: plan

- Approved by the owner: 2026-10-05 ("saa aloittaa V5"; decisions below, recommendations accepted)

- Scope: Build Master §13 (material flow), §14 (lifting and rigging), §39 (V5).
- Out of scope: the commercial layer, CRM, invoicing and internal charging between companies (V6); portals (V7); AI suggestions (V8).

## Build (§39)
Lift requests, lift plans, rigging allocation, lifting accessories, material tracking, cable drums and QR workflows. §39 lists no separate acceptance criteria. Proposed acceptance criteria:
1. A lift cannot be carried out without an approved lift plan.
2. Accessories whose inspection is due, and accessories overloaded for the lift, are detected.
3. Material and cable drum movements are traceable from delivery to installation and to the takt activity.
4. QR scanning works on a phone.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-05)
| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| 1 | Lift plans are approved by the **person responsible for lifting** (nostoista vastaava henkilö). |
| 2 | Cable drum length is tracked **in metres for every pull**. |
| 3 | QR labels are **generated by the system as PDF**. |

## Proposed details (recommendations)
- **Person responsible for lifting (decision 1):**
  - A new role template, **Lifting Supervisor** (*Nostovastaava*, ASSIGNED), carries the new permission `lift.plan.approve`. It is assigned per project as a project role, so each site has its own named approver.
  - The CEO and the Project Director hold the same right in all projects, so approval never stalls. Nobody else, including the Project Manager and Site Manager, approves lift plans.
  - The plan's author cannot approve their own plan.
- **Cable drums (decision 2):**
  - Each drum has a unique ID, manufacturer, cable type, original length, weight, dimensions, location, reserved activity, received date and inspection.
  - Every pull records metres, the takt activity, the date and the person, in an append-only log.
  - Remaining length = original length − pulled metres. A pull longer than the remaining length is refused, and a DB check prevents a negative remaining length.
- **QR labels (decision 3):**
  - A PDF label sheet (A4, several labels per sheet) is generated in the system for drums, material batches and lifting accessories.
  - The QR code contains only an internal URL with an opaque ID. It never contains data, so a scan outside the system reveals nothing.
  - Opening the URL requires signing in and company access, as with any other page.
- **Lift plan:**
  - Contents: load, weight, centre-of-gravity notes, crane, radius, crane capacity at that radius, accessories, rigging crew, area and safety distances, plus a link to a risk assessment (a document).
  - An approved plan is locked by a DB trigger, following the V3 baseline pattern. A change creates a new version.
  - When the lift is done, the completion is recorded.
- **Rigging allocation:** riggers and crane operators are booked with V4 bookings, so V4 conflict detection (overlap, trade) applies.
- **Lifting accessories:** a unique ID, working load limit (WLL) and next inspection date per accessory.
  - The lift plan refuses an accessory whose inspection is due.
  - The plan also refuses an accessory whose WLL is below the load.
- **Material tracking:** material batches move delivery → storage → workface → installed. Each batch links to a V4 delivery and a takt activity.

## Deliverables
- **Tables:** `lift_requests` (extends V4 logistics requests of type LIFT), `lift_plans` and `lift_plan_versions`, `lifting_accessories`, `lift_plan_accessories`, `material_batches`, `material_movements` (append-only), `cable_drums` and `cable_pulls` (append-only).
- **Pages:**
  - lifts: requests, plan, approval, completion;
  - accessory register;
  - material and drum register;
  - mobile scan view (camera QR, with typing the ID as a fallback);
  - QR label PDF.
- **New permissions (migration):** `lift.request`, `lift.plan.manage`, `lift.plan.approve`, `material.view` and `material.manage`, plus the new role template. The role matrix is presented in the release report.

## Migration impact
- New tables only.
- A new role template is added to existing companies by a migration, with an audit event.
- V1–V4 tables are not changed in a breaking way.
- PDF generation is a server-side library with no external service.
